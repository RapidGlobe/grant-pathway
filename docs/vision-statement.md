# Vision Statement — AI Grant Accelerator

> **To be the trusted, free writing companion for UK charities — helping non-specialists produce stronger, more consistent grant applications through AI-powered drafting, guideline summarisation, and mandatory human review.**

---

## Notes

- **"Trusted, free"** — reflects the donation ethos and completely free access model (DR-OD-002)
- **"Writing companion"** — positions the app as a supportive tool, not a replacement for human judgement
- **"UK charities"** — confirms the geographic and sector scope (DR-PS-003, DR-OD-003)
- **"Non-specialists"** — anchors the primary target user as volunteer or generalist staff (DR-TU-001)
- **"Stronger, more consistent"** — the measurable benefit to charities
- **"AI-powered drafting and guideline summarisation"** — reflects the two core AI capabilities (DR-AI-001)
- **"Mandatory human review"** — explicitly references the validation principle built into the product (DR-AI-003)
