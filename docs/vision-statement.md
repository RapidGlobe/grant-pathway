# Vision Statement — Grant Pathway

**Tier:** 3 — Stable; only update if a formal decision changed
**Volatility:** Low
**Update when:** The product vision is formally revised

> **To be the trusted, free preparation tool for UK charities — helping non-specialists produce stronger, more consistent grant applications through AI-assisted writing, plain-English guideline summarisation, and mandatory human review.**

---

## Notes

- **"Trusted, free"** — reflects the donation ethos and completely free access model (DR-OD-002)
- **"Preparation tool"** — positions the app as a preparation aid, not a submission platform (BD-01). Grant Pathway does not submit applications on behalf of charities, integrate with funder portals, or manage submission deadlines
- **"UK charities"** — confirms the geographic and sector scope (DR-PS-003, DR-OD-003)
- **"Non-specialists"** — anchors the primary target user as volunteer or generalist staff (DR-TU-001)
- **"Stronger, more consistent"** — the measurable benefit to charities
- **"AI-assisted writing"** — reflects the AI assists, not generates principle (BD-01 AI principle). Grant Pathway uses AI to help charities structure and clarify what they have already written — it does not generate answers on the charity's behalf. The charity writes every substantive answer; AI refines on request only
- **"Plain-English guideline summarisation"** — reflects the guideline summarisation capability (DR-AI-001)
- **"Mandatory human review"** — explicitly references the validation principle built into the product (DR-AI-003)
