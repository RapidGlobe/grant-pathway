# DR-PS-003 — Geographic Scope of Grant Coverage

## Question

Will the app support UK-only grants, or also EU and international grants?

## Context

Grant landscapes differ significantly by geography — UK grants have their own funders, terminology, eligibility rules, and regulatory context (e.g. Charity Commission England & Wales, OSCR in Scotland, CCNI in Northern Ireland). Supporting EU or international grants adds data complexity, legal variation, and a much larger knowledge base to maintain. Starting UK-only allows the app to go deep on a well-defined market. This decision also affects how the app is positioned and marketed.

## Options

- **Option A: UK only** — Focus exclusively on UK grant funders and programmes
- **Option B: UK primary, EU secondary** — UK grants in scope from day one, EU grants added in a later phase
- **Option C: UK and international from day one** — Broad coverage across multiple geographies at launch

## Decision

**Option A: UK only** — The app will support UK grants exclusively. EU and international grants are out of scope.

## Rationale

The UK grant landscape is rich enough to build a deeply valuable, expert tool without expanding geographically. UK-specific knowledge — funder conventions, charity law context, and application formats used by major UK funders — is a genuine differentiator. Charities applying to international funders tend to be larger organisations with existing grant-writing capacity, and are not the primary target audience. Staying UK-only also keeps future discovery scope manageable.
