# DR-GK-003 — Responsibility for Grant Database Maintenance

## Question

Who will be responsible for maintaining the grant database?

## Context

Someone must own the ongoing task of keeping grant information accurate, adding new funders, and removing closed programmes. This is an ongoing operational responsibility, not a one-off task. If the app is maintained by a single developer or small team, database maintenance may be a bottleneck. Outsourcing to volunteers, a charity partner, or a dedicated role each have cost and reliability trade-offs. This decision is related to DR-OD-001 (long-term ownership) and DR-GK-002 (how data is kept current).

## Options

- **Option A: The app's primary maintainer** — The same person(s) who build and run the app also maintain the grant data
- **Option B: A dedicated content role** — A separate person or team is responsible for grant data quality, either paid or volunteer
- **Option C: A charity sector partner** — Partner with an organisation (e.g. NCVO, a grant-making body) who takes on data stewardship
- **Option D: The user community** — Registered charities contribute to keeping data current through a moderated community process
- **Option E: Automated with human oversight** — Automated processes handle routine updates; a human reviews exceptions and additions

## Decision

**Deferred — not applicable to v1.** To be decided when grant discovery is scoped as a future phase (see DR-PS-002).

## Rationale

No grant database exists in v1, so there is no maintenance responsibility to assign. This decision depends on the source and currency decisions (DR-GK-001, DR-GK-002) and will be made alongside them when the discovery phase is planned.

## Date Decided
