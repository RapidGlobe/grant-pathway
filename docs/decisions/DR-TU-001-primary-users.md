# DR-TU-001 — Primary User Role

## Question

Who is the primary user of the app within a charity?

## Context

Different roles within a charity interact with grant applications differently. A grant writer focuses on content quality and funder requirements. A charity director cares about strategic fit and oversight. A finance officer is concerned with budgets and financial reporting. A volunteer may have limited time and experience. Designing for a single primary persona leads to a more coherent user experience. Secondary users can be accommodated later, but the primary user drives core UX decisions.

## Options

- **Option A: Grant writer / fundraiser** — The person whose job it is to research and write applications
- **Option B: Charity director / CEO** — Senior decision-maker who may also write applications in smaller charities
- **Option C: Finance officer** — Involved in budget sections of applications and post-grant reporting
- **Option D: Volunteer / non-specialist** — Someone with no dedicated fundraising role who takes on grant writing as one of many tasks
- **Option E: All of the above** — Design for multiple roles from the start

## Decision

**Option D: Volunteer / non-specialist** — The app is designed primarily for people with no dedicated grant-writing role or formal fundraising training.

## Rationale

The charities most burdened by grant applications are small organisations relying on volunteers. Designing for the non-specialist produces the most inclusive and impactful tool, and a well-designed simple tool remains usable by experienced fundraisers. This choice pushes the UX toward guided workflows, plain language, and AI that does more of the heavy lifting — all aligned with the app's donation ethos.
