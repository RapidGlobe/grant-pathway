# DR-LC-001 — Charity Sector Regulatory Compliance

## Question

Does the app need to comply with specific charity sector regulations, and if so, which ones?

## Context

Operating in the UK charity sector introduces regulatory considerations beyond standard software compliance. The app handles data about registered charities, may display information about grant funders, and produces content that influences funding decisions. Relevant frameworks may include UK GDPR and the Data Protection Act 2018, the Charity Commission's guidance on digital tools, and sector-specific codes of practice. If the app becomes widely used, it may also need to consider the AI regulation landscape (EU AI Act applicability, UK AI white paper). Understanding which regulations apply prevents legal risk and builds charity trust.

## Options

- **Option A: UK GDPR and DPA 2018 only** — Comply with standard UK data protection law; no sector-specific compliance required
- **Option B: UK GDPR + Charity Commission guidance** — Also follow Charity Commission guidance on digital tools and data handling for charities
- **Option C: Full sector compliance review** — Commission a legal review of all applicable sector-specific obligations before launch
- **Option D: GDPR + AI-specific compliance** — Also assess compliance with emerging UK/EU AI regulation (e.g. EU AI Act where applicable, UK AI white paper principles)

## Decision

**Option B: UK GDPR + Charity Commission guidance** — The app will comply with UK GDPR and the Data Protection Act 2018 as a baseline, and will follow Charity Commission guidance on digital tools and data handling. UK AI regulation developments will be monitored on an ongoing basis and reviewed annually or when significant regulatory changes occur.

## Rationale

UK GDPR compliance is non-negotiable and must be in place before launch. Following Charity Commission guidance on digital tools adds sector credibility and addresses the questions charities are most likely to ask. UK AI regulation is still developing — the proportionate approach is to monitor and be ready to adapt rather than attempting to comply with unfinished frameworks. A full legal review is not warranted for a v1 donated tool but should be considered before any significant scaling of the app.
