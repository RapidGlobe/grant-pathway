# DR-GK-001 — Source of Grant Information

## Question

Where will the app's information about available grants come from?

## Context

The quality of grant information directly affects how useful the app is. Sources range from open data platforms (360Giving, government portals) to paid databases (DSC's Funds Online, Idox Grantfinder) to manually researched content. Open data is free but may be incomplete. Paid databases are comprehensive but require licensing fees. Manual research is tailored but labour-intensive. The right source depends on the scope of grant coverage (see DR-PS-003) and whether grant discovery is in scope (see DR-PS-002).

## Options

- **Option A: Open data only** — Use freely available sources such as 360Giving, GOV.UK, and funder websites
- **Option B: Paid grant database licence** — License data from a provider such as DSC Funds Online or Idox Grantfinder
- **Option C: Manually researched and curated** — A small team researches and writes up grant listings directly
- **Option D: Community-contributed** — Charities and users contribute and update grant information (Wikipedia-style)
- **Option E: Combination** — Use open data as a base, supplemented by manual curation for priority funders

## Decision

**Deferred — not applicable to v1.** To be decided when grant discovery is scoped as a future phase (see DR-PS-002).

## Rationale

Grant discovery is out of scope for v1 (DR-PS-002, DR-IN-002). No grant knowledge base is required until discovery is built. Preferred future direction is Option E (combination of open data via 360Giving and manual curation for priority funders), as noted in DR-IN-002.

## Date Decided

_Deferred — to be revisited when discovery phase is planned._
