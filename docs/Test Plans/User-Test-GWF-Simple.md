## Before you start

You will need:

1. **Your charity details** — name, registration number, and a short description of what your charity does. You will be asked to type these in when you set up your profile.
2. **The Garfield Weston guidelines PDF** — this is the file the AI will read to work out the application questions. You can find it at:
   `docs\Grant Org Guidelines\garfield-weston-foundation-guidelines.pdf`
   (on the computer where this document is saved)
3. **The website address:** `grant-pathway-three.vercel.app` — open this in your web browser.

## Tests

---

### Test 1 — Create your account

**What you are testing:** Registering and verifying a new account.

**Steps:**

1. Go to `grant-pathway-three.vercel.app`
2. Click **Get started** or **Register**
3. Enter your name, email address, and a password you will remember
4. Click **Create account**
5. Check your email inbox — you should receive a verification email within a minute or two
6. Click the link in the email to verify your account
7. You should be taken back to Grant Pathway and see a welcome screen or dashboard

**Expected result:** You are signed in and can see the main dashboard or a prompt to set up your charity profile.

---

### Test 2 — Set up your charity profile

**What you are testing:** Entering your charity's details so the AI can personalise its responses.

**Steps:**

1. If you see a prompt to **Set up your charity profile**, click it. Otherwise, look for **Charity Profile** in the top navigation.
2. Fill in:
   - Your charity's **registered name**
   - Your charity's **registration number** (from the Charity Commission — shown on your charity's public page)
   - A paragraph describing **what your charity does** — who you help, how you help them, and where you operate. The more detail here, the better the AI's responses will be.
3. Click **Save**

**Expected result:** Your profile is saved and you can continue.

---

### Test 3 — Start a new application

**What you are testing:** Starting a new grant application and selecting the funder.

**Steps:**

1. From the dashboard, click **Start new application** (or **New application**)
2. On the next screen, search for **Garfield Weston Foundation** in the funder search box
3. Select **Garfield Weston Foundation** from the list
4. In the **Grant name** field, type something like: `Community Programme 2026`
5. Click **Continue**

**Expected result:** You are taken to Step 2 — the guidelines upload screen.

---

### Test 4 — Upload the guidelines

**What you are testing:** Uploading the grant guidelines PDF so the AI can read them.

**Steps:**

1. On the guidelines screen, click **Upload a file**
2. Find and select the file: `garfield-weston-foundation-guidelines.pdf`
   (in the `docs\Grant Org Guidelines` folder)
3. Wait for the upload to confirm — you should see a green tick or a confirmation message
4. Click **Continue**

**Expected result:** You are taken to Step 3 — the AI summary screen.

---

### Test 5 — Generate the AI summary

**What you are testing:** The AI reading the guidelines and identifying the application questions.

**Steps:**

1. On Step 3, click **Generate summary**
2. Wait — this usually takes 15–35 seconds. You will see a loading indicator.
3. Once it finishes, you should see a green banner saying something like **"We found 3 application questions in these guidelines"** and a list of the questions below.

**Expected result:** A summary appears with 3 questions listed. Note how long it took (roughly).

**Time taken:** **\_** seconds

---

### Test 6 — Confirm the preparation checklist

**What you are testing:** The preparation checklist screen that appears before you start writing.

**Steps:**

1. Click **Continue to Step 4**
2. You should see a screen called **Before you begin writing** listing documents to have ready (annual accounts, budget, etc.)
3. Read through the checklist items
4. Click **I have what I need — start writing**

**Expected result:** You are taken straight to the Q&A writing screen showing the 3 questions. _Note: if you see "No specific questions found" instead, please record this as a Fail and describe what you see._

---

### Test 7 — Write an answer yourself

**What you are testing:** Typing your own answer to a question.

**Steps:**

1. Find the first question on the screen
2. Click inside the answer box and type a short answer — even just a few sentences describing what your charity does in relation to that question
3. Watch the word count update as you type
4. Click somewhere outside the box — your answer should save automatically (no Save button needed)

**Expected result:** The word count updates as you type, and your answer stays when you click away.

---

### Test 8 — Use the AI to help with an answer

**What you are testing:** Asking the AI to improve or draft an answer for you.

**Steps:**

1. Find the second question on the screen
2. You can either type a short draft first, or leave the box empty
3. Click **Help me improve this** (below the answer box)
4. Wait a few seconds — the AI will write or improve your answer
5. Read the suggested answer — does it sound like it is about _your_ charity, based on the profile you set up?
6. If you are happy with it, click **Approve**

**Expected result:** The AI generates a relevant answer. It should refer to your charity's focus area (from your profile), not generic text.

---

### Test 9 — Complete remaining answers and proceed to Step 5

**What you are testing:** Finishing the Q&A and moving to the assembly screen.

**Steps:**

1. Complete or approve answers for any remaining questions
2. Once all questions have a green tick (approved), look for the **Continue** or **Next step** button
3. You may see a **senior review** confirmation screen — read it and click **Yes, continue**
4. You should be taken to Step 5

**Expected result:** You reach Step 5 — the export screen.

---

### Test 10 — Export the draft as a Word document

**What you are testing:** Downloading your draft answers as a Word document.

**Steps:**

1. On Step 5, click **Download Word document** (or similar)
2. Open the downloaded file
3. Check that it contains your answers — does it look like a proper grant application draft?

**Expected result:** A Word document downloads and opens correctly, containing your answers against each question heading.
