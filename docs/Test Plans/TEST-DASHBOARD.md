## Governance/Reserves Items Coverage by Funder Guidelines

Per `docs/PRD decisions/PDR-AI-008-governance-fact-detection-and-fallback.md`, the app has 5 fixed governance/reserves facts it can extract from a funder's guidelines and show as application items:

1. Total annual expenditure
2. Reserves level or reserves policy
3. Trustee-relatedness (whether any trustees are related to each other)
4. Bank-signatory count (how many people must authorise the charity's bank account)
5. Bank-signatories-relatedness (whether bank signatories are related to each other)
