# DDR-VI-002 — Favicon

## Question

What should the Grant Pathway favicon look like?

## Context

The favicon appears in the browser tab alongside the page title, in bookmarks, and when a user saves the site to their home screen. It is displayed at very small sizes (16x16px, 32x32px, 180x180px for Apple touch icon) so it must be simple, recognisable, and work as a single flat graphic. It should be consistent with the brand identity decided in DDR-VI-001.

The favicon is a minor but visible brand touchpoint. For a free tool used by charity workers who may bookmark the application, it contributes to perceived professionalism.

## Options

- **Option A — Teal "GP" monogram:** The letters G and P in white on a teal #0D6E6E square background. Simple, readable at small sizes, clearly brand-linked.
- **Option B — Simplified logo mark:** A reduced version of the logo graphic from DDR-VI-001 (if an icon exists). Works well if a standalone icon has already been designed.
- **Option C — Teal pathway/arrow symbol:** A simple abstract mark (e.g. a rightward arrow or path symbol) in teal or white on teal, evoking the "pathway" concept. More distinctive but requires original graphic design work.
- **Option D — Amber "G" initial:** A single letter G in Inter Bold, amber #D97706, on a teal background. Simpler than Option A; stands out in a crowded tab bar.

## Decision

**Option B -- The amber "G" person icon mark (extracted from the existing logo).**

The standalone icon portion of the Grant Pathway logo -- the amber "G" with person figure -- will be used as the favicon, placed on a teal (#0D6E6E) background square.

**Asset requirements before development:**
- The icon mark must be extracted from the logo file as a standalone transparent PNG or SVG.
- Favicon sizes required: 16x16px, 32x32px, and 180x180px (Apple touch icon).
- At 16x16px the person figure detail will simplify to a small amber dot on the G form -- this is acceptable; the amber-on-teal colour read is sufficient at that size.
