Community Lunch Club — The Henry Smith Charity

Your draft answers

Review and edit each answer before continuing. These are starting points — check every detail before use.

1 Describe your organisation, its mission and the communities it serves.
Helpers & Friends is a registered charity (no. 1234567) based in Barnsley, South Yorkshire. Founded in 2003, we support isolated older adults living alone in rural communities by providing weekly companionship visits, practical help, and connections to statutory services. We currently support 240 beneficiaries across seven villages, working with a team of 60 trained volunteers.

Review before using — check all facts, figures, and details are correct.

2 What will you use the funding for, and how will it benefit your beneficiaries?
This funding will support our Community Lunch Club programme — twice-weekly gatherings providing hot meals and social connection for up to 30 isolated older adults in Penistone. The club is the only regular social provision in the area. With your support, we will maintain two sessions per week for 12 months, covering food costs, venue hire, and volunteer expenses.

Review before using — check all facts, figures, and details are correct.

3 How many people will directly benefit, and over what period of time?
Over 12 months, 45 individuals will directly benefit from the Community Lunch Club. Each person attends an average of 60 sessions per year. The programme also has indirect benefits for family members and carers, reducing respite demands on approximately 30 households.
