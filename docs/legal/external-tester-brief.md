# Welcome to Grant Pathway — a note before you start testing

Thank you for agreeing to try Grant Pathway. This short note covers what to expect, so anything you notice reads as useful feedback rather than a surprise.

---

## What Grant Pathway is

Grant Pathway is a free tool that helps UK charities write grant applications. It uses AI to summarise a funder's guidelines in plain English, and — on request — to help you improve the structure and clarity of answers you've already written. You write every answer yourself; the AI never generates one from scratch.

## This is a pre-launch test build

You're using our working build ahead of public launch, not a finished product. A few things follow from that:

- **It may change while you're using it.** We're actively fixing and improving things during this test period. If something you saw yesterday looks different today, that's expected — let us know either way.
- **Your test data may not be kept long-term.** Please don't enter sensitive information you wouldn't want in a test environment. If you're not testing with your charity's real details, feel free to make that obvious in the charity name (e.g. prefix it "TEST —") — it helps us tell test data apart from the real thing.
- **This isn't a guarantee of production-level uptime.** It's generally reliable, but this is a test period, not a live commercial service yet.

## Two things you'll likely run into

**A monthly AI usage limit.** Each account gets 50 AI-assisted actions per calendar month (requesting a summary, asking AI to improve an answer). This resets automatically at the start of each month. You'll see a banner once you're close, and a clear message if you reach it — everything else about the tool keeps working, you just won't get AI assistance until it resets.

**An eligibility check that can stop an application outright.** If Grant Pathway detects a clear mismatch between your charity's profile and a funder's eligibility criteria, you'll see a red warning and won't be able to continue with that particular application — this is a deliberate safeguard, not a bug, and there's no way to override it once triggered. If it ever fires when you genuinely believe your charity is eligible, **please tell us** — that's exactly the kind of edge case we most want to hear about during this test.

## Your data

The full Terms of Service and Privacy Policy apply to your use of the test build, same as they will at launch — you can read them at `/terms` and `/privacy` on the site. In short: you own your content, we don't use it to train AI models, and you can delete your account (and everything in it) at any time from within the service.

## Giving feedback

Whatever you notice — confusing wording, something that felt slow, a mismatch that shouldn't have fired, or just a "this was great" — please email **wjokhia@rapidglobe.com**. There's no formal bug-report process; a plain email describing what you did and what you expected is exactly right.

Thank you again for taking the time to help us get this right before launch.
